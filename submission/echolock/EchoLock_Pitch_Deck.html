03Threat scenario · user · harm

The attack we stop: a fraudster clones the customer's voice and calls the bank to change their account details.

Why changing data is the real prize
Change one field and the attacker gets every control built on it. The real customer often learns days later.
Sensitive requests we hold
Registered mobile / email changeNew device or SIM bindingTransfer / UPI limit increaseAddress change + card reissueAdd beneficiary / nomineeUnblock card or account
Who's involved
  • Primary user: contact-centre agent. Has to decide while the caller is on the line.
  • Fraud analyst. Reviews flagged cases and needs evidence, not a bare score.
  • Protected person: the account holder. Not on the call and has no idea it's happening.
04Where to defend

Why we guard the bank's contact centre, not the customer's phone.

Detecting on the customer's phone
Not possible, and wouldn't help with this attack
  • Third-party apps can't capture call audio. Android limits VOICE_CALL capture to system apps (CAPTURE_AUDIO_OUTPUT). Google Play banned Accessibility-API call recording in May 2022, and iOS has no call-audio API.
  • Wrong place for this attack. The fraudster calls the bank, not the victim.
  • Privacy red flag. Recording or uploading every personal call clashes with the DPDP Act and Play policy.
  • Coverage gaps. Protection would depend on millions of installs, phone models and OS versions. Feature-phone users get nothing.
Detecting at the bank contact centre
Legitimate audio, the right moment, one integration
  • The bank owns the call line. Audio already passes through its IVR and agent systems under existing call-recording notices. No phone permissions needed.
  • The bank owns the action. The change request lands here, so we can hold it before it runs.
  • The bank owns the duty. RBI's 2025 authentication directions allow risk-based additional checks. That's our hook.
  • One deployment covers every customer, on any phone, with an independent registered channel back to the real person.
What about fake "bank official" calls to customers? That's a different attack: the fraudster calls the customer, and phone apps still can't hear the call. It's better solved with verified-caller signals (TRAI 1600-series numbers, an in-app "Is my bank calling me?"). We list it as a future extension, not our PS-01 scope.

06Solution & differentiation

Three layers: Detect, Confirm, Review. If one fails, the others still stop the attack.

LAYER 1
Detect cloned speech
  • A pretrained self-supervised anti-spoofing model (wav2vec 2.0, NII AntiDeepfake) runs on the bank's call audio.
  • Scores 4-second speech windows, tuned for narrowband phone audio.
  • Returns an honest UNKNOWN when audio is too short or too noisy. It never guesses "genuine".
LAYER 2
Confirm the exact change
  • Every sensitive change is sent to the customer's already-registered bank app.
  • The app shows the exact action, including the full new number ("••3210 → 98765 48876"). Signed, single-use, 60 s expiry.
  • A clone can copy a voice. It can't copy a registered device plus biometric unlock.
LAYER 3
Human review with evidence
  • A flagged voice opens a case even after an app approval. Contact and limit changes also wait a 24-hour cooling-off, so a coached customer has time to cancel.
  • The analyst sees reason codes, a score timeline and approval status, then calls back on the registered number.
  • The bank keeps final authority. The model never runs an action itself.

07Core detection & decision logic

Rolling evidence from one strong model, gated by quality, with a decision policy that can't fail open.

Rules that override the model
  • Action and call context (number match, SIM swap, repeats) come from bank systems.
  • The confirmation prompt starts at once. It never waits on the detector.
  • No freeze, blacklist or report from a voice score alone.
  • "Lost my phone" / no app: call can only protect (block card, freeze). Re-register via branch, video-KYC or net banking + 24 h hold.
  • τ is set per codec on a speaker-disjoint dev split at 1% false alarms, then frozen.

Why the confirm layer is essential
Error rate (EER) of wav2vec-small, the exact model we run:
Phone codecs keep errors under 9%, but packet loss + noise pushes it to 22%. So τ is set per codec, poor audio is UNKNOWN, and app confirmation carries the safety guarantee.
Ours: 960 test clips, 70 speakers. Kathbath real vs IndicSynth clones (XTTS-v2, FreeVC), call-level. Published: NII model card.
What we found · details in A·1–A·3
  • 1,727 clips × 6 channels scored; 22 ms per window on a laptop GPU
  • τ must be per codec: it moves from −2.27 (G.711) to +1.13 (clean)
  • XTTS-v2 clones are harder than FreeVC on every channel
  • On our own voices: 1.7% false alarms clean, 16% on G.711, so τ is re-set on each bank's calls in shadow mode
  • 26/26 confirm-layer tests pass: replay, tamper, expiry, wrong key
  • Still to measure: UNKNOWN rate, time-to-alert, analyst load

09Privacy, security & responsible AI

If we're wrong, the customer gets a verification step, never an accusation.

Minimal data
WE PROCESS
Call audio: a ≤16 s rolling buffer in memory, cleared when the call ends. Nothing new is archived.
WE STORE (FLAGGED CASES ONLY)
Score timeline, reason codes, model version, approval status. Encrypted, 7-day TTL.
WE NEVER COLLECT
Voiceprints, transcripts, contacts, OTP / PIN / card data, or anything from the customer's phone.
When the model is wrong
False alarm
A genuine caller just approves in the app. The analyst releases with a reason. No freeze, no "fraud" label.
Missed clone
Still needs the real customer's app approval, so the change can't run.
Unknown
Poor audio or a worker outage falls back to the normal secure route. It never fails open to "voice OK".
Fairness
TTS and speech-impaired users take the same tap path, with no disability disclosure. False alarms audited per language, gender and channel.
Misuse & adversaries
  • Adaptive attackers: codec and noise augmentation; Layer 2 holds even if the model is beaten.
  • Replay / tampering: nonce, action digest, 60 s expiry, single use.
  • Coached customers: app asks "Is anyone on a call telling you to do this?" A yes goes to review. Contact and limit changes wait 24 h, with alerts and one-tap cancel.
  • Score forging: only authenticated model workers can submit scores.
  • No surveillance: no bulk API and no public cloning service. Consent-only test clones.

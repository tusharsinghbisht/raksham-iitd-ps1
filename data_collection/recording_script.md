# EchoLock voice recording script

Each person records **6 short recordings** with their phone's voice recorder, about **10 minutes** in total.
Only our own team members are recorded. Everyone signs or OKs consent first: *"I agree my voice is recorded and cloned only for the EchoLock RAKSHAM project, kept privately, and deleted after the event."*

---

## Before you start

- **Recorder settings:** pick the highest quality. Choose **WAV** or lossless if your app offers it; otherwise the highest-bitrate setting. Use the **same phone** for all 6 recordings.
- **Room:** quiet room with the fan and AC off. No speakerphone, no earphones, no noise-cancel mode.
- **Phone position:** on a table or in your hand, **20–30 cm from your mouth**. Keep it still.
- **How to read:** natural pace and your normal voice, the way you'd speak to a bank agent. Don't act or over-pronounce.
- **Between sentences:** stay **silent for about 2 seconds**. We split the file automatically at those pauses.
- **If you stumble:** stop, delete that recording, and redo the whole block. Each block is only 1–2 minutes.
- **File names** (rename after recording): `S01_REF-EN`, `S01_REF-HI`, `S01_EN`, `S01_HI`, `S01_HG`, `S01_FREE`.
  Speaker IDs: Tushar = **S01**, Reedhan = **S02**, Shobhit = **S03**, Shikhar = **S04**.
- Upload the files to our **private** team folder, never a public link.

> **Update, 12 Oct 2026:** S02 (Reedhan) and S03 (Shobhit) later agreed to publish their raw recordings in this repository for research on clone detection. See `data_collection/README.md`.

## Recording 1 · `REF-EN` · English passage (≈60 s)

*Read in one go. This is the sample the cloning tool learns your voice from.*

> Every morning, the market near our house wakes up slowly. The tea seller lights his stove first, and the smell of ginger and cardamom drifts across the road. By seven o'clock, buses are honking, schoolchildren are running with heavy bags, and shopkeepers are pulling up their shutters with a loud metallic rattle. My grandmother says the city was much quieter thirty years ago, when bicycles outnumbered cars and people knew every neighbour by name. Today, most of us pay for vegetables by scanning a code on our phones, and we rarely carry cash. It is convenient, but it also means our phones hold almost everything about us: our money, our messages, and even our voices. That is why I believe technology should protect people quietly, without making their everyday lives harder.

## Recording 6 · `FREE` · speak freely (4 × about 15 s)

*No reading. Answer each prompt in any language, the way you'd normally talk. Pause about 3 seconds between answers.
This tests whether the detector raises false alarms on natural, unscripted speech.*

1. Describe what you did this morning.
2. Explain to a bank agent, in your own words, why you need to change your phone number. *(Make up the reason.)*
3. Talk about your favourite food and why you like it.
4. Give directions from a metro station to a place you like. *(Don't use your real home address.)*

---

### Do not

- Say real phone numbers, account numbers, PINs, OTPs or your real address.
- Record anyone outside the team, or put the recordings or any clones on public links or online cloning websites.
